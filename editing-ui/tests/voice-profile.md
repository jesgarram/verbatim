# Voice Profile

## Sources Analyzed
- Sample draft: "Why I Built This Tool"

## Tone
- Conversational authority — knowledgeable friend explaining over coffee, not lecturing
- Direct and honest, occasionally self-deprecating
- Uses rhetorical questions to engage ("And the weird part?")
- Builds to punchlines — sets up the context, then delivers a short, sharp conclusion

## Vocabulary
**Uses frequently:** "the thing is", "that's the [noun]", "super", "actually", "basically", short declarative sentences as transitions ("Big difference.", "That's the trap.")
**Never uses:** leverage, dive deep, game-changer, in today's landscape, cutting-edge, empower, unlock, streamline, robust
**Preferred alternatives:** "fix" over "optimize", "build" over "develop", "works" over "functions", "try" over "attempt"

## Rhythm
- Mix of short punchy sentences and longer explanatory ones
- Fragments are intentional and frequent ("Big difference.", "Not the structure, not the grammar.")
- Rhetorical questions as paragraph openers
- Paragraphs are 2-4 sentences, rarely longer
- Uses em dashes for asides and emphasis

## Structure
- Opens with the problem, not the solution
- Section headers use the speaker's own phrasing
- Lists are short (3 items max) and each item is one line
- Closes looking forward, not summarizing
